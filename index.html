Mobile control plane for OpenWRT

Your router,
under control from your pocket.

Devices, Wi‑Fi, firewall, QoS, parental control and traffic insights — a beautiful, privacy‑first companion app for your OpenWRT router.

Android · Free & Pro iOS · Free & Pro Tablet · optimized layouts Languages · EN + SK

What you can do

📡Devices

See every connected device, its signal, connection type and online status at a glance.

📶Wi‑Fi

Toggle radios, edit SSIDs and passwords, tune channels and bandwidth without touching LuCI.

🛡️Firewall & QoS

Port forwards, traffic rules and bandwidth limits — quick actions with full control.

👪Parental control

Schedules, internet pauses and per-device blocks. Pro unlocks unlimited profiles.

🩺Diagnostics

Ping, traceroute, DNS lookups and system log — troubleshoot straight from the sofa.

📊Traffic insights

Live bandwidth plus per-device history with export (Pro).

Privacy first, by design

No telemetry, no analytics, no accounts. The app talks straight to your router — credentials never leave your device.

  • Router credentials stored locally, sealed with AES‑256‑GCM encryption
  • Direct connection to your router — no cloud middleman
  • No advertising SDKs, no tracking
